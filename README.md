Xycopters: The Modular Building Game



You play as a worker in a modular building factory, tasked with constructing various buildings using given materials.



Game Overview


Xycopters is a browser-based game that challenges you to build and manage your own modular building empire. With a unique blend of strategy and creativity, the game offers endless hours of fun and relaxation.



Critical Controls



  
 Mouse or Keyboard control

  
Navigate through the factory menu using mouse wheel (optional)





How to Play


To start, you'll need to navigate to the game's home page and create an account. Once logged in, you can begin constructing buildings by adding materials and stacking them on top of each other.



Objective


The goal is to build and manage your factory efficiently while maintaining a balance between production, quality, and profitability.



You'll need to prioritize your building projects based on factors such as material requirements, labor costs, and potential revenue. As the game progresses, you'll unlock new materials, tools, and technologies to aid in your construction efforts.
